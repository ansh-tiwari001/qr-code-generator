Privacy Policy

This policy explains how QRTools handles information when you use the website.

Information we collect

QRTools does not require an account and does not maintain a user profile or user account database.

QR generation and third-party services

Some QR code tools use third-party QR generation services to create QR images. Information you enter into a generator may therefore be sent to that service as part of the QR image request. QRTools does not control the privacy practices of those third-party services.

Do not enter passwords, financial information, private documents or other sensitive information into a QR generator.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

QRTools does not currently require cookies for account login. If analytics or advertising services are added, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant technologies and third-party providers.

External links

The website may contain links to external services. QRTools is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website develops. Changes will be posted on this page.
