Frequently Asked Questions

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes, all tools are completely free.

Do I need to sign up?

No signup or login required.

Are QR codes permanent?

Standard QR codes never expire. You can use our AutoExpire QR tool to create time-limited QR codes.

Yes, use our Custom QR Designer tool.

Yes.

Is my data stored?

QRTools does not require an account or maintain a user profile database. Some generators use a third-party QR image service, so information entered into those generators may be included in the QR image request. Do not enter sensitive information.

Yes, fully responsive.

Yes.

Yes.
